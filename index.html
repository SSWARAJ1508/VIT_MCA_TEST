General Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before starting the exam:

  1. Total duration of the examination is 120 minutes (2 hours).
  2. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination.
  3. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself. You will not be required to end or submit your examination.
  4. There are 100 questions in total across 3 sections: Mathematics (10), English (10), and Computer Science (80).
  5. Each correct answer carries +1 mark. There is no negative marking for wrong answers.
  6. Do not refresh the page or try to open new tabs. This is a proctored environment. Copy-paste and right-click have been disabled.
